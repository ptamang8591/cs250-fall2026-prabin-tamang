BUG 1
  Function: total_treats
  Line number: 36 (inside total_treats loop)
  What was wrong: It used = instead of +=, overwriting `total` with each pet's treat count instead of adding them all up.
  My fix: Changed `total = treats_per_pet[name]` to `total += treats_per_pet[name]`.
  How I found it: I watched `total` in the debugger and was surprised when it dropped from `5` to `1` instead of increasing to `6` on the next pet.


BUG 2
  Function: average_age
  Line number: 44 (inside average_age loop)
  What was wrong: `range(1, len(ages))` started at index 1 instead of 0, skipping the first age (`3`) in `ages`.
  My fix: Changed `range(1, len(ages))` to `range(0, len(ages))`.
  How I found it: I watched `i` and `total` step through the loop and noticed `i` started at `1` so `ages[0]` (`3`) was never added to `total`.


BUG 3
  Function: find_oldest
  Line number: 55 (inside find_oldest loop)
  What was wrong: The condition checked `age < oldest_age` instead of `age > oldest_age`, looking for the youngest/smallest age rather than the oldest.
  My fix: Changed `if age < oldest_age:` to `if age > oldest_age:`.
  How I found it: I watched `age`, `oldest_age`, and `oldest_name` and was surprised that `oldest_age` stayed `0` and `oldest_name` stayed `""` instead of updating to `Pixel` (age `12`).
